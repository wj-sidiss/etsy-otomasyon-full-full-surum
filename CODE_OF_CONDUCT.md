# Code of conduct

This is a small project. The rules are short.

## What is expected

Be straightforward and be kind. Assume the other person is acting in good faith and
knows things you do not. Critique code, not people. When you disagree, say what you
would do instead and why.

Most people here are shop owners, not professional programmers. A question that seems
obvious to you cost someone real effort to ask in public. Answer it, or say nothing.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Comments demeaning someone for their identity, background, or level of experience
- Publishing anyone's private information — including a credential someone pasted into
  an issue by mistake. Tell them privately to rotate it; do not repeat it.
- Deliberately derailing discussions or repeatedly ignoring maintainer decisions

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and commit messages — anywhere the
project is being worked on.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub's private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/wj-sidiss/etsy-otomasyon-full-full-surum-2/security/advisories/new),
which also serves as a private channel for conduct reports. Reports stay confidential.

The maintainer will respond as they are able. This is an unfunded side project, so
response times vary — but reports are read.

## Consequences

Depending on severity: a request to stop, edited or removed comments, or a block from
the repository. The maintainer decides, and will explain the reason.

---

This document is deliberately brief. For a fuller standard many projects adopt, see the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
